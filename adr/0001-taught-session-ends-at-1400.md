# ADR-0001 — The taught session ends at 14:00 and hands off

**Date:** 2026-09-22 (recorded 2026-09-23, reconstructed from the day's discussion)
**Status:** accepted

## Context

The workshop is a full day at the Penn Epigenetics Institute. The Vahedi lab had
already built an afternoon of hands-on material — five exercise cards and four pod
challenges — on their own datasets, in their own repository.

The obvious plan was a single continuous day under one instructor. That would have
meant learning someone else's exercises well enough to teach them, two days before
delivery, and taking responsibility for material we did not write and had not run.

## Decision

The taught session runs 10:00–14:00 and ends with an explicit handover. The afternoon
is the Vahedi lab's, self-directed, with their TAs.

We must not build teaching material that depends on their exercises, and we must not
present their afternoon as a continuation of ours.

## Consequences

Good: the preparation problem collapses to something achievable in two days. The
division of labour is legible to attendees — different people, different material,
after lunch. Their afternoon gets a better-prepared room than it would have.

Bad: the day has a seam in it, and a seam is a place people leave. The handover slide
has to make the afternoon sound worth staying for without overselling material we
don't own.

Also: our exercises must stand alone. Anything that depends on their repository is a
fallback, never a requirement — which is what [ADR-0002](0002-exercises-are-menus.md)
settles.
